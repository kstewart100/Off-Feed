House rules

Every video in an issue has to pass all five.

  1. Made by a person on camera or in the edit, with their own argument or craft.
  2. Ten minutes or longer. No Shorts, no clip compilations.
  3. No reaction videos, recaps, or AI-narrated rehashes.
  4. At most one video per channel per issue.
  5. Every link is checked against YouTube before it goes in.
